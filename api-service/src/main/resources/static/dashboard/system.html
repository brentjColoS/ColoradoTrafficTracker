Current operational state

Architecture is only useful when its signals are trustworthy

Repeated speeds can be normal. Health is based on successful observation freshness, complete incident snapshots, and provider state—not whether the values changed.

What the states mean

Healthy
Both corridors have recent usable flow, CDOT snapshots are fresh, and the provider guard is reporting normally.
Degraded
The site can still serve traffic data, but at least one named check is outside its expected threshold.
Out of service
No monitored corridor has recent usable flow. One exhausted TomTom account is not an outage while another account can ingest.

Low-maintenance monitoring

The server reports externally twice daily. It allows one hour for flow and CDOT freshness, warns when off-site backup evidence is more than eight days old, and warns at 10 GB of available disk space.

Every degraded or failure state includes the observed condition and a concrete next action.
