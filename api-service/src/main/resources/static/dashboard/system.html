System

From provider telemetry to explainable traffic state

Colorado Traffic Tracker turns live road speeds and CDOT reports into a clear view of current and past conditions on I-25 and I-70. It keeps history, handles imperfect source data, and explains when information may be delayed.

Spatial projection

Raw provider geometry becomes evidence-backed corridor state.

Provider paths are clipped to the monitored roads, projected against route geometry and marker anchors, then aggregated into stable half-mile identities with coverage provenance.

What the states mean

Healthy
Both corridors have recent usable speeds, CDOT reports are fresh, and the traffic source is reporting normally.
Degraded
The site can still show traffic, but at least one named check is late or unavailable.
Out of service
No monitored corridor has recent usable speeds. One unavailable TomTom account is not an outage while another can continue.

Background monitoring

The server reports externally twice daily. It allows one hour for traffic and CDOT freshness, warns after eight days without an off-site backup, and warns at 10 GB of available disk space.

Every warning includes what happened and a next action.
