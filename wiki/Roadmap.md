![Colorado Traffic Tracker](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/brentjColoS/ColoradoTrafficTracker/main/docs/assets/ctt-wordmark.svg)

The current site collects and retains traffic observations, serves a live and
historical dashboard, and supports maps, incident context and matched baseline
comparisons. The [source history](https://github.com/brentjColoS/ColoradoTrafficTracker/commits/main/)
records shipped changes.

## Questions I want to investigate

The next substantial comparison is winter traffic on I-70: how the drive home
differs between snowy weekends and clear ones, where slowdowns form, and how
departure time changes the afternoon. The
[Why page](https://coloradotraffictracker.net/dashboard/why.html) explains that
motivation.

## Ongoing work

Keep collection within its provider allowances, preserve queryable history,
make missing or estimated information clear, and maintain the tests and recovery
checks as the application changes. These are continuing responsibilities rather
than a promise that the platform is finished.

The README graphics and this wiki provide a shorter route into that work.
[Architecture](Architecture) and [Testing and verification](Testing-Strategy)
are useful starting points for inspecting the implementation.
